Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Data we collect

None. Speaker Cleaner does not collect, transmit, or store personal data on any server. The app has no account system, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no crash reporting that leaves the device.

2. Data stored on your device

The app stores only a small amount of local state: whether you have completed onboarding, whether the free Water Eject run has been used, and your purchase entitlement. Generated audio files are written to the app's temporary cache and are not uploaded anywhere.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The app does not receive or store your payment information. Purchase entitlement is read from StoreKit and cached locally so the app works offline.

4. Audio and permissions

The app plays sound through the device speaker. It does not request access to the microphone, photos, contacts, location, or health data.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
