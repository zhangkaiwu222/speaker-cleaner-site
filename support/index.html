Support

Does this repair a broken speaker?

No. The app only plays sound waves that may help move water or loose dust out of the speaker chamber. It does not repair hardware. Do not use it with a damaged speaker.

Why can't I hear anything?

Set the iOS system volume to maximum before starting. The app cannot change system volume for you. Make sure the phone is not connected to headphones or Bluetooth audio devices.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the paywall screen and tap “Restore purchase”. The entitlement is read from your Apple account.

Safety

Do not wear headphones. Do not use if the speaker is damaged. Each run is limited to 60 seconds and the peak amplitude is capped at 0.18 (about −15 dBFS).
